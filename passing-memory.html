Late light on South Congress

The Story

There's a stretch of South Congress that only works for about twenty minutes a night — the sun drops behind the shopfronts and the whole street turns the color of a warm bulb. Placeholder writing to show how a longer note reads underneath the frames.

Some entries will have this. Most won't. When the words are there, this is where they live — plain, personal, no pressure to fill the space. Swap this out with whatever you actually felt like saying that day.

The point of the page is the light and the frames; the writing is a quiet companion, not the headline. Keep it short, keep it honest, and let the photographs carry the weight.
